# CPA Uganda design brief

## 1. Product purpose
Manage members, savings, shares, loans and double-entry accounts for CPA Uganda's single-branch SACCO.

## 2. Primary user
The branch administrator and accounting staff doing daily financial work in a browser; dense tables must remain easy to scan.

## 3. Principles
- Keep amounts readable and aligned with tabular numerals.
- Use plain headings and the existing menu icons.
- Keep the owner's black, blue and white palette.
- Give headings a clear visual lead over labels and supporting text.

## 4. Success metric
Staff can find the key balances and move between their daily tasks without clipped headings or unreadable tables.

## 5. Out of scope
- Accounting rules and report calculations.
- Navigation restructuring.
- PDF typography changes.

## 6. Learned constraints
- **2026-10-28** — Use Google Sans for the web interface. The owner disliked Nunito Sans and asked for more energy, using a rounded geometric dashboard reference; they specifically identified Google Sans as the preferred direction. Bundle the fonts locally and keep emphasis in the heading and figure hierarchy.
- **2026-10-28** — Dashboard balance labels (Members, Savings, Shares, Loans outstanding, Loan interest owed, Cash & bank) use small uppercase text with positive tracking. Use restrained Google Sans weights: regular copy, medium labels/large figures/section headings, semibold totals and attention states. The owner found the previous dashboard weights too heavy and competing.
